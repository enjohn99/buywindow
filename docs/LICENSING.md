# Licensing direction

BuyWindow is intended to have a community/self-hosted edition and a paid hosted offering.

Two models remain under consideration before public release:

1. **AGPLv3 + separate commercial license** — preserves an OSI-open-source community edition while offering a proprietary commercial license for organizations that do not want AGPL obligations.
2. **Source-available commercial model (for example a BSL-style structure)** — allows broader commercial restrictions, but should not be marketed as open source until the applicable change license/date makes it so.

No final license text is included in v0.1. Contributors should not be accepted until ownership/contributor terms and the dual-licensing strategy are finalized with qualified legal review.
